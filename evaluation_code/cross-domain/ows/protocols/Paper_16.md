## **2. Synthesis & Post-Processing**

### 2.1 Synthesis Protocol
1. **PbS Quantum Dots Synthesis**:  
   1. Dissolve **lead acetate** in **oleic acid** and **1‑octadecene** within a nitrogen‐filled glovebox to ensure an inert atmosphere.  
   2. Degas the reaction mixture overnight to eliminate oxygen and moisture.  
   3. Heat the solution at **100 °C** to fully dissolve the precursors, then raise the temperature to **150 °C**.  
   4. Rapidly inject a pre‑prepared solution of **hexamethyldisilathiane** (mixed with 1‑octadecene as the sulfur precursor) to initiate nucleation of oleic‑acid‑capped PbS QDs.
   
2. **ZnO Nanoparticles Synthesis**:  
   1. Prepare a solution using **zinc acetate dihydrate** and **potassium hydroxide** in methanol.  
   2. Maintain the reaction under a constant temperature of **60 °C** for **2.5 hours** to allow controlled precipitation and growth of ZnO nanoparticles.

### 2.2 Post-Treatments & Modification
1. **PbS QD Purification & Ligand Exchange**:  
   1. Precipitate the synthesized PbS QDs by adding a mixture of butanol and ethanol, then perform centrifugation to separate the nanoparticles from the reaction solvent.  
   2. Re-disperse the precipitated QDs in hexane and execute a second precipitation using acetone, ensuring cleaner quantum dot surfaces.  
   3. Re-disperse the purified QDs in octane at a concentration of **50 mg/ml** to yield a stable colloidal solution.  
   4. Conduct a solid‑state ligand exchange using **tetrabutylammonium iodide (TBAI)** and **1,2‑ethanedithiol (EDT)** to remove the native oleic acid ligands, thereby improving charge transport and enhancing device performance.
