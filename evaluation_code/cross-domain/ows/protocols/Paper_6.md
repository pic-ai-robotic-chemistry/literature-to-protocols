### 2.1 Synthesis Protocol
1. **Solid–Liquid Synthesis (Sample A)**: Mix equimolar amounts of Bi₂O₃ and V₂O₅ in **0.5 M nitric acid**. Stir the suspension at **room temperature** for **4 days** to enable complete dissolution and reaction.
2. **Solid–Liquid Synthesis (Sample B)**: Dissolve **10 mmol of Bi(NO₃)₃·5H₂O** and **5 mmol of V₂O₅** in **0.75 M nitric acid**. Maintain stirring at **room temperature** for **2 days** to promote homogeneous precursor mixing.
3. **Solid–Liquid Synthesis (Sample C)**: Combine equimolar amounts (5 mmol each) of Bi₂O₃ and V₂O₅ in **1 M acetic acid**. Stir the mixture at **room temperature** for **16 days** to form the BiVO₄ precursor solution.

### 2.2 Post-Treatments & Modification
1. **Air Calcination**: For Sample C, subject the obtained BiVO₄ powder to **air calcination at 400 °C for 1 hour** to improve crystallinity and phase formation.
2. **Substrate Deposition**: Deposit the dried BiVO₄ powder onto conductive fluorine-doped tin oxide (FTO) substrates via dropcasting. This step is performed to enhance interfacial charge transport and optimize the photocatalyst’s electronic properties.

### 2.3 Co-catalyst & Surface Functionalization

2. **Process Adaptation**: Ensure that the co-catalyst addition is carried out after substrate deposition so that the discrete catalytic phase is optimally interfaced with the base BiVO₄ material, providing targeted improvements in catalytic activity.

The synthesis protocol leverages distinct acid media and reaction durations to tailor material properties.
