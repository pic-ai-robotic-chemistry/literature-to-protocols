## **5. Testing System Configuration**

## **6. Performance Evaluation & Validation**

### 6.1 Activity Metrics
- **H₂ Evolution Rate**: The optimized PTI-550 photocatalyst achieved a hydrogen evolution rate of 189 μmol·h⁻¹ under UV illumination (365 nm), with the reaction conducted in pure water and no sacrificial agents.
- **O₂ Evolution Rate**: The corresponding oxygen evolution rate was 91 μmol·h⁻¹ under identical conditions.
- **H₂:O₂ Stoichiometric Ratio**: The molar ratio of evolved H₂ to O₂ was maintained at approximately 2:1 throughout the reaction, consistent with the theoretical stoichiometry for overall water splitting.
- **Apparent Quantum Yield (AQY)**: An AQY of 8% was reported at 365 nm, determined by quantifying the evolved H₂ and O₂ under monochromatic irradiation and calculating the ratio of generated electrons to incident photons.
- **Facet-Performance Correlation**: The overall water splitting activity exhibited a linear relationship with the surface area ratio of prismatic {1010} to basal {0001} facets, as determined by statistical analysis of crystal aspect ratios.

- **Facet-Dependent Reactivity**: Theoretical calculations and experimental mapping (HAADF-STEM, EDS) confirmed that both reduction (H₂ evolution) and oxidation (O₂ evolution) occur on the prismatic {1010} facets, with selective photodeposition of Pt and Co co-catalysts enhancing charge separation and reactivity.
- **Charge Transfer Pathway**: Temperature-dependent photoluminescence (TD-PL) measurements indicated an exciton binding energy of 50.4 meV, suggesting efficient exciton migration along the conjugated layers toward the reactive facets.
