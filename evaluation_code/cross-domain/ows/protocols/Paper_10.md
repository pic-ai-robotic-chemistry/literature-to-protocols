## **2. Synthesis & Post-Processing**

### 2.1 Synthesis Protocol
1. Dissolve **12.6 g** of Ga(NO₃)₃·nH₂O in **200 mL** of the chosen aqueous medium – either de-ionized water for Ga₂O₃(DI) or a CaCl₂ ultra-pure water solution for Ga₂O₃(UP-Ca(0.001)) (ensuring consistent water quality).  
2. Slowly add ammonium hydroxide to the solution to adjust the pH to **9** (this hydroxylation facilitates controlled precipitation).  
3. Allow the resulting Ga-hydroxide precipitate to form and then perform vacuum filtration to separate the solid from the liquid.  
4. Calcine the dried precipitate at **1273 K for ten hours in air** to crystallize the Ga₂O₃ phase.

### 2.2 Post-Treatments & Modification
1. For the Ga₂O₃(UP-Ca(0.001)) variant, introduce Zn doping by incorporating a Zn precursor to achieve **3 mol%** Zn (this modification is intended to tailor the catalyst’s electronic and surface properties).  
2. Mix the Zn precursor thoroughly with the precipitate prior to, or immediately following, filtration to ensure homogeneous distribution before the calcination step.

### 2.3 Co-catalyst & Surface Functionalization
1. Prepare an aqueous solution containing the Rh₀.₅Cr₁.₅O₃ precursors and disperse the calcined Ga₂O₃ support (either undoped or Zn-doped) into this solution.  
2. Perform photodeposition by irradiating the suspension with a UV-Vis light source (using a high-pressure Hg lamp, e.g., **450 W USHIO UM-452**, under conditions as previously established) to deposit **0.5 wt%** of Rh₀.₅Cr₁.₅O₃ onto the support.
