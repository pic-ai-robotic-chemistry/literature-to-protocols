## **2. Synthesis & Post-Processing**

### 2.1 Synthesis Protocol
1. Clean and load the FTO-coated glass substrate into the magnetron sputtering chamber under an argon atmosphere.  
2. Deposit a **30 nm** CuO interfacial layer using RF sputtering at a low power setting of **25 W** (to reduce interfacial recombination losses).  
3. Co-sputter the CuO:Al layer using stoichiometric CuO targets and an Al target (99.99% purity); control the Al doping by adjusting the Al sputtering power between **0 and 20 W** (optimized at **12 W**) while maintaining the CuO target power at **25 W**.  
4. Deposit the n-type ZnO:Al layer with a thickness of **20 nm** using RF sputtering at a power of **300 W**, with Al incorporation controlled up to **30 W** to tune the doping level.

### 2.2 Post-Treatments & Modification
1. Subject the as-deposited films to rapid thermal annealing at **550 °C for 1 minute** immediately after sputtering (to enhance crystallinity and stabilize the phase).  
2. Maintain an inert atmosphere during the post-deposition treatment to prevent unwanted reduction reactions (e.g., to avoid the reduction of CuO to Cu₂O).

### 2.3 Co-catalyst & Surface Functionalization
1. Deposit a **20 nm** nanocrystal-engineered TiO₂ protective layer by RF sputtering, adjusting the sputtering power within a range of **50 to 200 W** to ensure optimal crystallinity and photocorrosion resistance.  
2. Sputter Au–Pd nanoparticles from a Au–Pd (60:40) target onto the TiO₂ layer, ensuring a uniform deposition that serves as co-catalysts for enhanced photoelectrochemical activity.  

This sequential record captures the base material fabrication via RF sputtering with precise control over chemical precursors, sputtering power, and thermal treatment, followed by the deposition of a protective layer and co-catalyst functionalization—all engineered to optimize photocurrent density and long-term stability for overall water splitting.

## **6. Performance Evaluation & Validation**

### 6.1 Activity Metrics
- **Photocurrent Density (Jsc)**: Achieved a record-high 5.4 mA cm⁻² at 0 VRHE under AM 1.5G illumination (100 mW cm⁻²) in 0.1 M Na₂SO₄ (pH 5.84), using a p-(CuO/CuO:Al)/n-ZnO:Al/TiO₂/Au–Pd photocathode with a 200 W-sputtered TiO₂ layer.
- **Hydrogen Evolution**: Generated 60 mmol H₂ over 6 hours at an average photocurrent of 0.16 mA, with a faradaic efficiency of 98% (calculated as: Faradaic efficiency = (nH₂) / (Q / (Z·F)), where F = 96,485 C mol⁻¹).
- **H₂:O₂ Stoichiometric Ratio**: Inferred to be 2:1 based on the two-electrode setup and high faradaic efficiency, confirming overall water splitting.
- **Photovoltage**: Enhanced to 0.53 VRHE, attributed to the engineered p–n heterojunction and optimized interfacial layers.
- **Incident Photon-to-Current Efficiency (IPCE)**: Exceeded 20% between 400–650 nm, peaking at 50% at 550 nm, measured under monochromatic illumination.

### 6.2 Selectivity & Mechanism
