### 6.1 Characterization Results

#### 6.1.1 Crystal Structure and Phase Purity
- **Description:** The crystal structure and phase purity of the MOF materials were determined using powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD) and single-crystal X-ray diffraction (SCXRD). PXRD is essential for confirming bulk phase purity and crystallinity, while SCXRD provides detailed structural information, including lattice type, space group, and sublattice arrangement.
- **Findings:** For α‐MUF‐9, PXRD patterns matched the simulated pattern derived from SCXRD data, confirming high phase purity and crystallinity. SCXRD revealed that α‐MUF‐9 adopts a primitive cubic (pcu) lattice in the P4̄3m space group, a structural motif retained throughout secondary growth and interpenetration. In MUF‐91 and MUF‐92, SCXRD allowed direct refinement of the occupancy (PIP) of the secondary sublattice, reaching 70% and 74% respectively after 9 hours of secondary growth. For MUF‐93, SCXRD and anomalous dispersion experiments confirmed the exclusive incorporation of cobalt into the secondary [Co₄O(bpdc)₃] sublattice during early growth.
- **Observations:** The retention of the P4̄3m space group across all frameworks indicates robust structural stability. PXRD peak changes (e.g., decrease at 2θ = 5.2° and increase at 2θ = 7.3°) tracked the progression of interpenetration, while the absence of impurity peaks confirmed phase purity.

### 6.2 Performance Results

#### 6.2.1 Partial Interpenetration Percentage (PIP)
- **Description:** The partial interpenetration percentage (PIP) quantifies the occupancy of a secondary, interpenetrating sublattice within a host MOF framework. It is defined as the fraction of the secondary sublattice (e.g., [Zn₄O(bpdc)₃], [Zn₄O(bpdc–NH₂)₃], or [Co₄O(bpdc)₃]) incorporated into the host structure, as determined by single-crystal X-ray diffraction (SCXRD), powder X-ray diffraction (PXRD), and ^1H NMR spectroscopy.
- **Experimental Data:**
  - MUF-91: PIP of [Zn₄O(bpdc)₃] reaches up to 70% after 9 hours of secondary growth at 95 °C.
  - MUF-92: PIP of [Zn₄O(bpdc–NH₂)₃] is approximately 74% after 9 hours under similar conditions.
  - MUF-93: PIP of [Co₄O(bpdc)₃] attains ~70% after 60 hours in large crystals and up to 80% in microcrystalline samples after 18 hours.
  - MUF-101: PIP of [Zn₄O(L2)₃] is maintained at ~7–15% to maximize porosity for catalytic applications.
- **Observations and Trends:** PIP increases with reaction time and is tunable by adjusting growth conditions. High PIP values correlate with enhanced framework stability and controlled pore modification, while moderate PIP in MUF-101 preserves porosity for catalysis.

#### 6.2.2 Framework Stability and Pore Modification
- **Description:** Framework stability refers to the retention of crystallinity, space group, and pore structure during and after secondary growth. Pore modification is assessed by the successful incorporation of a secondary sublattice without significant displacement of the original linkers or metal nodes.
- **Experimental Data:** SCXRD and PXRD confirm that the host frameworks (e.g., α‐MUF‐9) retain their P4̄3m space group and crystallinity throughout secondary growth. ^1H NMR analysis of digested samples shows minimal displacement of L1 linkers.
- **Observations and Trends:** The frameworks remain structurally robust up to high PIP values. After extended growth, a shell of the secondary phase may form on the crystal surface, but the core structure and PIP remain unaffected.

#### 6.2.3 Catalytic Activity and Enantioselectivity (MUF-101)
- **Description:** Catalytic activity is evaluated by the enantiomeric excess (ee) achieved in asymmetric reactions, measured by high-performance liquid chromatography (HPLC). Enantiomeric excess is calculated as:
  \[
  ee = \frac{|A_{R} - A_{S}|}{A_{R} + A_{S}} \times 100\%
  \]
  where \(A_{R}\) and \(A_{S}\) are the HPLC peak areas of the respective enantiomers.
- **Experimental Data:**
  - Aldol reaction (2-chloro-5-nitrobenzaldehyde + acetone, ~3% catalyst loading): (R)-MUF-101 yields ee = −13.9%; (S)-MUF-101 gives the opposite sign.
  - Henry reaction (2-chloro-5-nitrobenzaldehyde + nitromethane, 4% catalyst loading): (R)-MUF-101 yields ee = −9.4%; (S)-MUF-101 yields ee = +10.2%.
- **Observations and Trends:** The sign of ee reverses with the host’s chirality, confirming chiral induction. Control experiments with non-chiral hosts or molecular catalysts yield 0% ee, highlighting the unique performance of the hetero-interpenetrated, chiral MOF.

#### 6.2.4 Metal Incorporation Selectivity (MUF-93)
- **Description:** Selective incorporation of metal ions into the secondary sublattice is assessed by anomalous dispersion X-ray experiments and atomic absorption spectroscopy.
- **Experimental Data:** In MUF-93, cobalt is incorporated almost exclusively into the [Co₄O(bpdc)₃] sublattice during the first 60 hours, with significant exchange into the host sublattice only after 168 hours.
- **Observations and Trends:** Kinetic control and low precursor concentrations enable precise tuning of metal composition, supporting the design of functionally distinct pore environments.
